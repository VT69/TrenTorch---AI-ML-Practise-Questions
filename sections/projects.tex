\resumeEntry{AlphaVol-NeuralHAR --- Sentiment-Augmented Volatility Forecasting}{\href{https://alphavol-neuralhar.streamlit.app}{alphavol-neuralhar.streamlit.app}}
\begin{resumeList}
    \item Co-authored a research paper (preprint submitted to Journal of Empirical Finance) extending the HAR-RV volatility model with FinBERT sentiment and a hybrid Neural-HAR architecture (FT-Transformer + Gated Residual Network) across three asset classes (BTC, SPX, NIFTY50; \char`\~8,300 daily observations, 2015--2026), aggregating sentiment data from multiple news APIs; Neural-HAR achieved statistically significant QLIKE forecast improvements over the HAR baseline for all three assets (Diebold-Mariano test, $p < 0.05$) and was the sole survivor of the three-model Confidence Set (HAR, HAR-S, Neural-HAR) for SPX and NIFTY ($\alpha = 0.10$).

\end{resumeList}
