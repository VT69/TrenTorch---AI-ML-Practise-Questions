\resumeEntry{AlphaVol-NeuralHAR --- Sentiment-Augmented Volatility Forecasting}{\href{https://github.com/VT69/AlphaVol-NeuralHAR-Global}{github.com/VT69/AlphaVol-NeuralHAR-Global}}
\begin{resumeList}
    \item Co-authored a research paper (preprint submitted to Journal of Empirical Finance) extending the HAR-RV volatility model with FinBERT sentiment and a hybrid Neural-HAR architecture (FT-Transformer + Gated Residual Network) across three asset classes (BTC, SPX, NIFTY50; \char`\~8,300 daily observations, 2015--2026), aggregating sentiment data from multiple news APIs; Neural-HAR achieved statistically significant QLIKE forecast improvements over the HAR baseline for all three assets (Diebold-Mariano test, $p < 0.05$) and was the sole survivor of the three-model Confidence Set (HAR, HAR-S, Neural-HAR) for SPX and NIFTY ($\alpha = 0.10$).
    \item Validated economic significance via a volatility-targeting backtest (Quarter-Kelly sizing, 5bps slippage, $\leq$5$\times$ leverage), showing Neural-HAR reduced maximum drawdown by up to 7 percentage points vs.\ the HAR baseline (SPX: $-$35.2\% vs.\ $-$42.3\%) despite no Sharpe-ratio gain; independently identified and corrected a front-end-alignment bug in the evaluation pipeline that had inverted a significance result, ensuring full reproducibility of reported findings.
\end{resumeList}
